# Module 4 · Risk & Mindset

Knowing the mechanics is half the game. Staying calm and avoiding silly mistakes is the other half — often the more important one.

## 1. Understanding risk {#risk}

**Risk** simply means: the value can go down, not just up. Shares can grow your money faster than a bank — but in exchange, their price wobbles, and sometimes falls a lot for a while. That trade-off is the deal, and it's perfectly normal.

Two simple ideas make risk manageable:

- **Only invest money you won't need soon.** Money you'll need in a year or two shouldn't be in shares — a dip at the wrong moment could force you to sell at a loss.
- **Time reduces the bumpiness.** Over a single day the market is a rollercoaster. Over 10–15 years, the ride has historically smoothed out and trended upward. Long horizons are your friend.

> [!NOTE] Risk isn't the enemy
>
> The goal isn't zero risk — that just means zero growth. The goal is *sensible* risk: enough to grow, never enough to hurt you if things go wrong.

## 2. Common beginner mistakes (so you can skip them) {#mistakes}

| The trap | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| **Acting on "hot tips"** from WhatsApp, YouTube or a relative. | If a stranger really had a guaranteed winner, why would they tell you? Tips are how beginners get trapped. |
| **FOMO** — buying something just because it's shot up and everyone's talking about it. | You often arrive right at the top, just before it falls. |
| **Panic-selling** when prices drop. | It locks in a real loss. Drops are normal and usually temporary. |
| **Putting everything in one stock.** | If that one company stumbles, your whole savings stumble with it. |
| **Borrowing to invest / "leverage" / F&O.** | Magnifies losses fast. This is advanced, high-risk territory — not for beginners. |
| **Checking prices ten times a day.** | It spikes your stress and tempts you into needless buying and selling. |

## 3. Fear & greed — the two emotions that cost the most {#emotions}

Markets are driven by two feelings, and both push you to do exactly the wrong thing:

- **Greed** shows up when prices are high and everyone's excited — tempting you to pile in at the top.
- **Fear** shows up when prices crash and the news is gloomy — tempting you to sell at the bottom.

![Markets play a cruel trick: excitement runs hottest right before a fall, and gloom is darkest right before a recovery. Feeling the crowd's mood — and gently leaning the other way — is half the battle.](../assets/img/diagrams/04-risk-and-mindset-1.svg)

Buy-high, sell-low is how people lose money — and it's driven by emotion, not logic. The famous advice from investor Warren Buffett:

> [!TIP] Worth remembering
>
> "Be fearful when others are greedy, and greedy when others are fearful." In plain words: don't follow the herd. A boring, automatic monthly SIP quietly sidesteps both emotions for you — which is a big part of why it works.

## 4. Spotting scams & bad tips {#scams}

Sadly, the money world attracts tricksters. Most scams wave the same red flags. Treat any of these as a loud alarm bell:

- 🚩 **"Guaranteed returns"** or "double your money in 3 months." Nobody can guarantee market returns. Nobody.
- 🚩 **Pressure to act *right now*** before a "limited" chance disappears.
- 🚩 **Tips from strangers** in Telegram/WhatsApp groups, or a "sure-shot" caller.
- 🚩 **Unregistered advisors.** Genuine advisors are **SEBI-registered** — you can check.
- 🚩 **Anything you don't understand.** If you can't explain how it makes money, don't put money in.

> [!WARNING] The golden test
>
> If it sounds too good to be true, it is. Real investing is slow and a little boring. Excitement and urgency are sales tricks, not investment strategy.

## 5. Golden rules to remember {#rules}

If you forget everything else on this site, keep these:

1. **Invest only what you won't need soon** — and never borrowed money.
2. **Start small, start early.** Time is your biggest advantage.
3. **Spread it out.** Never bet everything on one stock.
4. **Think in years, not days.** Patience is the real superpower.
5. **Ignore tips and hype.** Understand what you own.
6. **Stay calm in crashes.** They're normal; selling in panic is what does the damage.
7. **Automate it** with a monthly SIP, then mostly leave it alone.
8. **Keep learning, slowly.** You don't have to know everything to begin — just enough to start safely.
