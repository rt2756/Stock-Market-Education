# Learning the Stock Market 📈

Simple, friendly notes to learn investing from scratch — no jargon, no pressure. Read at your own pace.

![Day to day the market wobbles — green days, red days — but over the long run it has tended to drift upward. That patient, long-term view is the idea this whole site is built around.](assets/img/diagrams/home-1.svg)

> [!NOTE] How to use this
>
> Start with **Core Basics** and go in order — each builds on the last. You don't need any background; every new word is explained in plain language. The later groups (Going Deeper, For the Curious) are there for whenever you're ready — there's no rush to reach them. ☕

Everything here is built around the Indian stock market (the same world that [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/modules/) covers) — so you'll see the NSE, SEBI, Nifty, Demat accounts and rupees. The ideas, though, are the same everywhere.
