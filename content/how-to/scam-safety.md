# Scam Safety Checklist

Learning to spot a scam is just as important as learning to invest — this page shows you the warning signs and what to do if something feels wrong.

## 1. The one golden rule {#golden}

> [!WARNING]
>
> **If it sounds too good to be true, it is.**
>
> Real investing is slow and a little bit boring. Your money grows quietly over years, not overnight. Anyone who promises fast, guaranteed, or "secret" profits is trying to sell you something — usually a trap.
>
> Excitement and urgency are **sales tricks, not strategy**. A genuine opportunity will still be there tomorrow after you have had time to think. A scam needs you to act before you can.

## 2. Red flags checklist {#red-flags}

If you spot even one of these, stop and step back. Treat each one as a reason to walk away, not a detail to overlook.

- 🚩 **Guaranteed or "fixed" returns** — no honest investment can promise this.
- 🚩 **"Double your money"** promises (often "in a few days" or "in a month").
- 🚩 **Pressure to act NOW** — limited-time offers, "only a few seats left", countdown timers.
- 🚩 **Stock tips from strangers** on Telegram, WhatsApp or other groups you didn't ask to join.
- 🚩 **Unregistered "advisors" or "experts"** who can't show a SEBI registration number.
- 🚩 Being asked to **transfer money to a personal bank account or wallet** instead of your own broking account.
- 🚩 **Screenshots of huge profits** — these are trivially easy to fake.
- 🚩 Anyone asking for your **OTP, password or login** — no genuine person or company ever needs these.
- 🚩 **Apps or links sent directly to you** rather than downloaded from official app stores.

## 3. Common scams to know {#common}

Scams come in a few familiar shapes. Once you can name them, they are much easier to spot.

| Scam | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Pump & dump** | A cheap "penny stock" is hyped to drive the price up, then the schemers sell their shares on you and the price crashes. |
| **Ponzi / chain schemes** | "Returns" are simply paid from new joiners' money. It collapses the moment new people stop joining. |
| **Fake advisory / tips groups** | Unregistered groups charging fees for "sure-shot" calls that benefit only the people running them. |
| **Dabba trading** | Illegal off-exchange betting on prices, outside the real stock market and with no legal protection. |
| **Fake trading apps & websites** | Look-alike apps and sites that show fake "profits" but never let you withdraw your real money. |
| **"Recovery" scams** | Target people who already lost money, promising to get it back — for an upfront fee — then vanish. |

## 4. How to verify before trusting anyone {#verify}

Before you give anyone your money or your trust, take a few minutes to check. This habit alone will protect you from most scams.

- Check that any advisor or broker is **SEBI-registered** — you can verify on the SEBI website (sebi.gov.in).
- Use **only SEBI-registered brokers** to open your account and place trades.
- Your money should **only ever go to YOUR OWN trading/broking account** — never to a person, never to a private wallet.
- **Never share your OTP or passwords** with anyone, for any reason.
- Download apps **only from official sources** — your phone's official app store or the broker's own website.

## 5. If something feels wrong {#if-scammed}

If you are worried you have been targeted or have already paid, do not panic and do not feel ashamed — these scams are designed to fool careful people. Act quickly.

1. **Stop and don't pay more.** Do not send "one last payment", a "release fee", or a "tax" to get your money out — that is part of the trap.
2. **Save all evidence.** Keep chats, messages, screenshots, receipts, transaction IDs and phone numbers.
3. **Report it immediately** to:
  - **SEBI SCORES** for market-related complaints (scores.sebi.gov.in).
  - The **cybercrime helpline 1930** and the portal cybercrime.gov.in.
  - **Your bank** — call them at once to flag the transaction and try to freeze it.

## 6. Your quick safety checklist {#checklist}

Keep these habits and you will sidestep almost every trap out there.

1. Remember the golden rule: too good to be true means it isn't true.
2. Ignore urgency and "limited-time" pressure — give yourself time to think.
3. Never trust stock tips from strangers in Telegram or WhatsApp groups.
4. Verify that any advisor or broker is SEBI-registered before you trust them.
5. Send money only to your own broking account — never to a person or wallet.
6. Never share your OTP, password or login with anyone.
7. Download apps only from official app stores or the broker's own site.
8. If something feels wrong, stop, save evidence, and report it (SEBI SCORES, 1930, your bank).

> [!TIP]
>
> **Nobody legitimate guarantees stock-market returns** — not your bank, not SEBI, not even the world's best investors. The moment someone promises certainty, you are talking to a scammer.
