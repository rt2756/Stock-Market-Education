# Glossary (A–Z)

Every key term from across the notes, in one place — skim it, or use Ctrl/Cmd-F to search.

## A {#a}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AMC (Asset Management Company) | The company that runs a mutual fund and decides where your pooled money is invested. |
| Annual report | A yearly document a company publishes showing its profits, debts and plans, useful before you invest. |
| Ask price | The lowest price a seller is currently willing to accept for a share. |

## B {#b}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bear market | A long stretch when share prices are generally falling and investors feel gloomy. |
| Bid price | The highest price a buyer is currently willing to pay for a share. |
| Blue-chip | A large, well-known and financially strong company, such as those in the Nifty 50. |
| Bonus shares | Extra free shares a company gives existing shareholders out of its reserves. |
| Breakeven (options) | The level the underlying must cross for a bought option to profit: strike + premium for a call, strike − premium for a put. See [Options Explained](options-explained.html#example). |
| Broker | A SEBI-registered firm (like Zerodha or Groww) through which you buy and sell shares. |
| Bull market | A long stretch when share prices are generally rising and investors feel confident. |
| Buyback | When a company buys back its own shares from the market, usually returning cash to shareholders. |

## C {#c}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Call option | A contract giving you the right (not obligation) to buy a share at a fixed price by a set date. |
| Candlestick | A small chart shape showing a share's open, high, low and close price for a period. |
| Capital gain | The profit you make when you sell an investment for more than you paid for it. |
| Cash settlement | Settling a derivative by paying the cash difference instead of delivering shares — how index F&O settle at expiry. See [Expiry Explained](expiry-explained.html#settlement). |
| CDSL | One of India's two depositories that hold your shares safely in electronic (demat) form. |
| CNC / MIS / NRML | Zerodha's product tags when you place an order: **CNC** for delivery (shares you keep), **MIS** for intraday (closed the same day), **NRML** for carrying F&O overnight. See [Using Zerodha](using-zerodha.html#product). |
| Compounding | Earning returns on your past returns, so your money grows faster the longer you stay invested. |
| Corporate action | Any company event that affects its shares, such as dividends, bonus shares or a stock split. |

## D {#d}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Demat account | An account that holds your shares in electronic form, the way a bank holds your money. |
| Derivative | A contract whose value comes from an underlying asset like a share or index, such as futures and options. |
| Diversification | Spreading money across many shares or sectors so one bad pick does not sink everything. |
| Dividend | A share of a company's profit paid out in cash to its shareholders. |

## E {#e}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ELSS | An Equity Linked Savings Scheme, a tax-saving mutual fund with a 3-year lock-in under Section 80C. |
| Equity | Another word for shares, meaning part-ownership in a company. |
| Exit load | A small fee some mutual funds charge if you withdraw your money too soon. |
| Expense ratio | The yearly fee a mutual fund charges, shown as a small percentage of your invested amount. |
| Expiry | The fixed date a futures or options contract settles and ceases to exist. Index options expire weekly; the last weekly each month is the monthly expiry. Shares you own in delivery never expire. See [Expiry Explained](expiry-explained.html). |

## F {#f}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Face value | The original nominal value of a share set by the company, often ₹10 or ₹1. |
| FII / DII | Foreign and Domestic Institutional Investors — big players whose buying or selling moves the market. |
| Fundamental analysis | Judging a share by studying the company's business, profits and financial health. |
| Futures | A contract to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price on a future date. |

## H {#h}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hedging | Taking a protective position to reduce the risk of losses on another investment. |
| Holdings | Shares that have settled into your demat account and are yours to keep, shown separately from open intraday/F&O Positions in your broker app. See [Using Zerodha](using-zerodha.html#tabs). |

## I {#i}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Index | A basket of selected shares used to track how the overall market is doing, like the Nifty 50 or Sensex. |
| Index fund | A low-cost mutual fund that simply copies an index instead of trying to beat it. |
| Intrinsic value (options) | The "real" part of an option's premium — how far it is in the money right now (zero for OTM options). |
| IPO | An Initial Public Offering, when a company sells its shares to the public for the first time. |
| ITM / ATM / OTM | Moneyness — whether a strike is **I**n, **A**t or **O**ut of **T**he **M**oney versus the current price. See [Options Explained](options-explained.html#moneyness). |

## K {#k}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| KYC | Know Your Customer — the one-time identity check (PAN, Aadhaar) needed before you can invest. |

## L {#l}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Large-cap / Mid-cap / Small-cap | Company size groups by market value, from the biggest and steadiest to smaller and riskier. |
| Leverage | Using borrowed money to invest more than you have, which magnifies both gains and losses. |
| Limit order | An instruction to buy or sell only at a price you set, or better. |
| Liquidity | How easily a share can be bought or sold quickly without moving its price much. |
| Lock-in period | A fixed time during which you cannot sell or withdraw an investment, as with ELSS funds. |
| Long-dated options | Index options with quarter-end or half-year expiries stretching years out; they exist but trade thinly in India. |
| Lot size | The fixed number of units you must trade together in futures and options contracts. See [Options Explained](options-explained.html#lots). |
| LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gains) | Tax on profit from shares or equity funds held over one year. |

## M {#m}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Margin | Money you put up to borrow from your broker so you can take a larger position. |
| Market capitalisation | A company's total value, found by multiplying its share price by the number of shares. |
| Market order | An instruction to buy or sell immediately at the best price available right now. |
| Mutual fund | A pool where many investors' money is managed together by professionals for a fee. |

## N {#n}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NAV (Net Asset Value) | The per-unit price of a mutual fund, updated once at the end of each trading day. |
| Nifty 50 | The index of 50 large companies on the NSE that tracks the broad Indian market. |
| NSDL | One of India's two depositories that hold shares electronically, alongside CDSL. |
| NSE / BSE | India's two main stock exchanges — the National and Bombay Stock Exchanges where shares trade. |

## O {#o}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open interest (OI) | The number of F&O contracts currently open at a strike — a gauge of how busy and easy to trade it is. |
| Option | A contract giving the right, but not the duty, to buy or sell a share at a set price by a set date. |
| Option chain | The table of all strikes for one expiry — calls on one side, puts on the other, strikes down the middle. See [Options Explained](options-explained.html#chain). |
| Option writer | The seller of an option, who collects the premium and takes on the obligation — a small capped gain against a potentially huge loss. |

## P {#p}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P/E ratio | The price-to-earnings ratio, showing how much you pay for each ₹1 of a company's yearly profit. |
| Physical settlement | Settling a stock F&O contract held to expiry with actual shares instead of cash — a whole lot's worth, so it needs serious money. See [Expiry Explained](expiry-explained.html#settlement). |
| Positions | Trades that are open but not yet settled — today's intraday trades and open F&O — shown separately from your settled Holdings. See [Using Zerodha](using-zerodha.html#tabs). |
| Portfolio | The full collection of all the investments you hold. |
| Premium (options) | The price of an option itself, quoted per unit — you pay premium × lot size. See [Options Explained](options-explained.html#lots). |
| Primary market | Where companies sell brand-new shares to the public, mainly through IPOs. |
| Put option | A contract giving you the right (not obligation) to sell a share at a fixed price by a set date. |

## R {#r}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rebalancing | Adjusting your holdings now and then to return to your intended mix of investments. |
| ROE (Return on Equity) | A measure of how much profit a company makes from shareholders' money. |

## S {#s}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SEBI | The Securities and Exchange Board of India, the regulator that protects investors and oversees the market. |
| Secondary market | Where investors buy and sell already-issued shares among themselves, on the NSE and BSE. |
| Sensex | The BSE's index of 30 large, established companies that tracks the Indian market. |
| SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) | Investing a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month to build wealth steadily. |
| Square off | Closing an open position — selling what you bought, or buying back what you sold. |
| STCG (Short-Term Capital Gains) | Tax on profit from shares or equity funds held for one year or less. |
| Stop-loss | An order that automatically sells a share if its price falls to a level you set, limiting losses. |
| STT (Securities Transaction Tax) | A small tax charged by the government on every buy and sell of shares. |
| Strike price | The fixed price at which an option lets you buy or sell the underlying share. |

## T {#t}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Technical analysis | Judging a share by studying its price charts and patterns rather than the company itself. |
| Term insurance | Pure life cover that pays your family if you die during the policy term, with no investment mixed in. |
| Time decay (theta) | The daily loss of an option's time value as expiry approaches — it accelerates sharply near the end. |
| Time value (options) | The "hope" part of a premium, paid for the time left on the clock; it melts to zero by expiry. See [Options Explained](options-explained.html#inside). |
| Trading account | The account through which you place buy and sell orders, linked to your demat and bank accounts. |

## U {#u}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ULIP | A Unit Linked Insurance Plan that mixes life insurance with investment, often with high charges. |
| Underlying | The asset a derivative's value is based on — a stock, or an index like the Nifty. |

## V {#v}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Volatility | How much and how quickly a share's price swings up and down. |
| Volume | The number of shares traded in a given period, showing how active a stock is. |

## Z {#z}

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Zero-DTE (0DTE) | An option on its own expiry day ("zero days to expiry") — the cheapest, fastest and riskiest hours of its life. See [Expiry Explained](expiry-explained.html#zero-dte). |
