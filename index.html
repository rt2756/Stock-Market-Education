📘 Stock Market Notes Glossary

Learning the Stock Market 📈

Simple, friendly notes to learn investing from scratch — no jargon, no pressure. Read at your own pace.

How to use this

Start with Core Basics and go in order — each builds on the last. You don't need any background; every new word is explained in plain language. The later groups (Going Deeper, For the Curious) are there for whenever you're ready — there's no rush to reach them. ☕

Everything here is built around the Indian stock market (the same world that Zerodha Varsity covers) — so you'll see the NSE, SEBI, Nifty, Demat accounts and rupees. The ideas, though, are the same everywhere.
